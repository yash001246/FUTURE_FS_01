Our Story

Every bag has a line —
from crop to cup.

Roast Line started in a converted garage with a 1-kilo drum roaster and a notebook full of failed batches. Ten years on, the drum is bigger but the method hasn't changed: we buy direct from growers we've actually met, roast in small lots, and log every profile so the cup tastes the same whether it's your first visit or your five-hundredth.

The Roast Line

From green bean to
your cup, in five stops.

01

Sourced

Bought direct from growers in Chikmagalur, Coorg & beyond — full traceability, fair price.

02

Cupped

Every lot is tasted blind against three benchmark cups before it earns a place on our list.

03

Roasted

18kg drum, logged profile, tasted again mid-batch — nothing goes out untested.

04

Rested

48 hours minimum off the roaster before beans reach the grinder.

05

Poured

Ground to order, dialled in every morning by hand — no auto-doser, ever.
